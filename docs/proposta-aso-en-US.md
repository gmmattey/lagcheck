Description:
About to queue for ranked? Check your connection first.

Lagcheck measures the route from your iPhone to reference infrastructure and shows latency, jitter and packet loss in seconds. Download speed isn't what wins matches. Stability is.

It is not an official game-server ping. For console or PC, the result is an estimate of the connection in the same environment.

WHAT YOU GET
• A clear verdict: Ready for Ranked, I'd Avoid Ranked Right Now, or Unstable Conditions.
• Idle vs. loaded route: see whether your connection gets worse when other devices are using it (bufferbloat).
• Honest details: the symptoms we observed, without blaming your Wi-Fi, ISP or server unless there is evidence.
• Squad card: share your reading to Discord or any chat in one tap.

If there aren't enough valid UDP responses, Lagcheck says "inconclusive" instead of making up a score.

No account. No login. Run a Check before you queue.
